Zombie Sweeper – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Zombie Sweeper is a game made by an independent developer (contact below). This page explains what data the game uses. In short: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, settings and high scores are saved only on your own device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Online co-op and friends

When you use online co-op, the friends list or invitations, the game connects to our small relay server that passes game messages between players. For this the server handles:

  • a random player ID created by the game (not linked to your name, email or phone),
  • the player name you type in the game (max. 16 characters) and your friend code,
  • the player IDs of the friends you added,
  • your IP address, which is needed to deliver the messages.

The player ID, name, friend code and friends list are kept on the server so your friends list works next time. IP addresses are only used while you are connected and may appear in short technical server logs that are not shared and are regularly deleted. We do not sell or share any of this data with anyone, and it is not used for advertising.

Playing alone or co-op on the same Wi-Fi does not use the relay server.

Deleting your data

Email us with your friend code (shown in the game's Friends screen) and we delete your online entry. Deleting the game removes everything stored on your device.

Children

The game contains intense violence and is not meant for children under 17. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
